**Regeneration test** (`experiments/ml-benchmark/arms/2026-09-18-round3-rules-exemplars-sonnet5/`, 21 draws): 2 × 2 of rules × held-out exemplars, three draws per cell, on `matplotlib` (in-sample for the rules) **and** on `functions` (held out). In-sample the rules move median paragraph similarity to his text 0.845 → 0.93 and every round-3 signature lands; held-out they move similarity not at all (0.814 → 0.809) — the in-sample jump is largely the rules quoting his sentences. What transfers is what the rules state generically: comma density 0.56 → 0.72 against his 0.82. Exemplars: no measurable closeness effect in any pairing; without rules they pull style signatures part-way. Position: harmless, cheap, unproven. Two method findings worth keeping: the seed an editor edited is anchored to his text (0.951 vs 0.845 for fresh draws) and is not a baseline; character similarity is near its ceiling as an instrument — the editor-side series (touch rate, accepted lines, edit magnitude) is the better round-over-round measure.

**Addendum, same day — blind pairwise judge and the exemplar variants.** Character similarity was flat on the held-out lecture for every arm, so a second instrument was built: `judge.mjs` (Opus 5, random order, style-only, TIE allowed; 2,570 judgements, first-shown wins 0.48–0.54 = no bias). It detects the in-sample rule effect decisively (matplotlib 76 : 24) and, held out, a small consistent edge for the rules (55 : 45) and for rules + the shipped pairs (57 : 43, p = 0.054). Three exemplar variants were then generated (9 more held-out draws) and judged: contrastive triples 51 : 49, a 56-pair set 50 : 50, the 56-pair set without the new rules 50 : 50 (it reproduced the rules' comma density but not their judged style). Verdict: rules earn their place; the shipped small example set is the only form with any positive signal and it is not significant; larger or contrastive sets add nothing; the exemplar line is closed pending round-4 editor data. The contrastive `NOT:` rendering was a local build only and is not shipped. Method lesson: `pgrep -f "judge.mjs"` in a waiter loop matches the waiter itself — two hours lost to a self-deadlock; use a pid or a done-file. ml#13 was squash-merged (`a24e925`, trailer carried). #311 held for this verdict at Matt's call.
