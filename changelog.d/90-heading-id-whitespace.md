### Fixed

- **A whitespace-only edit to a heading no longer re-translates its section from scratch** (#90, defect 6). A section's id kept a trailing space as a trailing hyphen (`## Supply and Demand ` gave `supply-and-demand-`), and so did a space left beside stripped punctuation (`## Overview !` gave `overview-`). Trimming the space, as an editor that trims on save does, changed the id, so the diff detector saw one section deleted and another added. The added one was translated as new, and the existing translation was dropped with its subsections and every human refinement. Leading and trailing hyphens are now dropped from every id, so the trim is one modified section, updated from its translation. Ids are recomputed on every run and never compared across runs, so nothing needs migrating. Adding or removing a role in a heading still changes its id, as #90 records.
