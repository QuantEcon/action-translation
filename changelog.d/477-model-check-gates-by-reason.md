### Fixed

- **A diff check the model reports as failed now always routes the review to `editor`** (#477). A model-asserted check (`scopeCorrect`, `positionCorrect`) that came back `false` gated only through one `minor` `diff-check` finding, and `findings[]` keeps the worst 20: when 20 findings sorted ahead of it (20 `minor` translation findings, say, or 15 beside 5 diff-quality issues), that finding was cut, and the verdict read `auto-merge` (in shadow mode, `wouldAutoMerge: true`) beside a published `scopeCorrect: false`. Nothing merged, since review modes are only `off` and `shadow`, but it put false points into the shadow-mode calibration data. The failure now also gates by a `recommendationReasons` entry of its own, `model-asserted check failed: <check>`, worded apart from a deterministic check's `diff check failed: <check>` so calibration can still tell model opinion from measured fact. During the shadow window some verdicts move from `auto-merge` to `editor`; earlier verdicts that read `auto-merge` with a model check at `false` should be counted as `editor`.
